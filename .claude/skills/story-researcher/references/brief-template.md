# research.md template

```markdown
# <Title>
Mode: true story | fiction | inspired by
Length target: 30-60 s (about 2.2-2.8 spoken words per second)

## Angle (chosen)
One sentence: who wants what, what stops them, what changes.
Why this beats the alternatives: ...

## Facts
| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ... | verified / unverified / disputed / invented | URL or "none reachable" |

## Dramatised (not factual)
- Invented dialogue, inner thoughts, exact times, composite characters.

## Emotional arc
Hook feeling -> tension -> turn -> payoff feeling.

## Do not say
- Claims that are unverified or disputed and must not be stated as fact.
- Sensitive details (living private people, tragedies) that need care.
```
