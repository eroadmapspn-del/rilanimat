# Reel checklist

**Hook (0-3 s)**: starts on the first word (first word before 1.0 s); 12 words or fewer; a detail only this story has; the picture matches the line.

**Story**: one clear want, one obstacle, a turn near 60-70%, a payoff that answers the hook; last line is quiet, not a summary or a request.

**Pacing**: 2.0-3.2 spoken words/s; scenes 1.5-9 s; deliberate silences only; no scene visually identical to its neighbour; total 30-60 s.

**Captions**: word-level highlight in sync; at most 4 words per line; no word longer than 17 characters; inside 90 px side and 420 px bottom margins (Instagram's buttons and caption bar cover the lower area and right edge); high contrast on every scene.

**Transitions**: mostly fade or cut; slide/zoom only at turns; each 0.5 s or shorter; nothing covers a caption.

**Audio**: voice about -14 LUFS overall, music 12-18 dB under voice and ducking in speech, music fades in 1 s and out before the end, no clipping, no silence at the start or end longer than 1 s.

**Video file**: 1080x1920, 30 fps, h264, AAC audio, duration matches the composition, decodes without errors, under 90 s.

**Honesty**: sources recorded; stock and music licences noted; AI-generated assets labelled; placeholders replaced; fiction not presented as fact.
